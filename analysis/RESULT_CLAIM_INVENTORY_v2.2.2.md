## Current Truthful Status

The manuscript’s four empirical result tables are traceable to stored machine-readable artifacts. The synthetic table maps to `repeated_seed_smoke_v2`, not the older `repeated_seed_smoke` directory. The latter must be treated as a superseded failed/earlier smoke attempt and not cited by the manuscript.

The present paper is therefore **artifact-reproducible**, but not yet **fresh-rerun-validated as a complete release**. The remainder of this work will create an immutable manifest, rerun each exact protocol, recompute the summaries from the per-seed records, cross-check all manuscript values, and update the paper to remove or correct every unsupported claim.
