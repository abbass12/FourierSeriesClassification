# MDPI Mathematics Submission Requirements Verified on 2026-08-11

Source: https://www.mdpi.com/journal/mathematics/instructions

## Requirements relevant to this manuscript

1. A research manuscript should include title, author affiliations, abstract, keywords, Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions, Supplementary Materials, author-contribution statement, funding statement, data-availability statement, acknowledgments, conflict-of-interest statement, and references.
2. The Materials and Methods section must contain enough detail to enable replication, name software and versions, and state whether code is available.
3. Authors must disclose use of generative AI for text, data, graphics, study design, data collection, analysis, or interpretation. Superficial grammar/spelling/formatting assistance does not require disclosure.
4. Authors must make all associated materials, data, and protocols available to readers; code and data should, where possible, be deposited in a trusted repository.
5. A Data Availability Statement is required for all published articles.

## Implications

The current manuscript needs a complete reproducibility statement, software/version list, data-availability statement, author-contributions statement, conflict-of-interest statement, and a carefully reviewed generative-AI disclosure. The manuscript must not be submitted until these have been completed and the full experiments are rerun with multiple random seeds and statistical reporting.

## Caveat

The journal instructions do not guarantee editorial or peer-review acceptance. The document only establishes formal submission requirements.
